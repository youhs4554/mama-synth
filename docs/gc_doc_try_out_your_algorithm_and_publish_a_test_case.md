### Try out your algorithm and publish a test case[¶](#try-out-your-algorithm-and-publish-a-test-case "Permanent link")

After your algorithm has been deployed and your container image is active, you can test it on the platform. To do so, click *Try-out Algorithm* and upload a test case.

As an editor of the algorithm you can test and debug your algorithm in total 5 times per unique algorithm image. Once you have reached the limit, any extra jobs will be deducted from your personal algorithm credits, of which you get 1000 per month.

#### Publish a test case[¶](#publish-a-test-case "Permanent link")

We consider it best practice to upload at least one test case and make it public. To have a case with a result visible to the public, you should have a case with a successful run of the last container available in your *Results*. To make a successful particular run visible to the public, firstly, navigate to the result details by clicking on the *i* icon. Secondly, click on the *Edit this Result* button. Thirdly, check the *Public* box and press *Save*.
